# Onboarding for agent sessions

This is a startup guide for sessions explicitly assigned to a team by the owner
or a lead, not a second policy register. Ordinary contributors and their agents
follow [participation](../contributor-workflow.md#participation); an assignment
does not automatically make a session a standing coordinator.

Apply the [eleven standing coordination policies](../contributor-workflow.md#standing-coordinator-operation).

## Read before starting

1. Read [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) in full for safety, Git, validation, credential
   boundaries and Quality's PR/review authority.
2. Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md), then the
   [contributor workflow](../contributor-workflow.md), including
   [current owner policy](../contributor-workflow.md#current-owner-policy).
   Follow its link to the single Fleet register for standing decisions.
3. Read the canonical issue, dependencies, accepted ownership and applicable
   area instructions before editing. Follow
   [claiming work](../contributor-workflow.md#claiming-work); preserve explicit
   source, execution and publication holds.

## 1. Who does what

Use [status: running work](../status.md#running-work) for current role holders and
acceptance links, and [participation](../contributor-workflow.md#participation)
for the distinction between contributors, delegates and standing coordinators.
Resolve current contacts rather than retaining session or host maps here.
An acceptance transfers responsibility only, not permissions or Quality's
receipt, gate, rerun, PR-custody or arming authority.

## 2. Where things live

Use the workflow's [operational sources of truth](../contributor-workflow.md#operational-sources-of-truth)
to place scope, acceptance and head-specific evidence in the right records.

- **[#696](https://github.com/danswett/agent-bridge/issues/696), the rank:** Product's epics in order, plus the rules for changing the order. Work on what is ranked; if you can't find your work there, ask Product before starting.

## The first message

Reply to the assigning lead with your bounded scope and exclusions, canonical
issue, accepted ownership, own worktree/branch, permitted validation and next
deliverable. State unresolved dependencies or conflicts using the workflow's
[conflict procedure](../contributor-workflow.md#conflicting-or-unavailable-information).
Obtain explicit acceptance before starting; silence is not consent.

For review and publication, submit evidence through
[readiness and review evidence](../contributor-workflow.md#readiness-and-review-evidence)
and [AGENTS.md's PR rules](../../AGENTS.md#pull-requests), not a checklist copied
here. Leave a [handoff](../contributor-workflow.md#handoffs) when pausing or ending
the assignment. This guide creates no watcher, polling or reporting cadence and
releases no held phase.
