Close up: the 48-unit drawings at 56 px, on a dark row

Drawn from pwa/design/sprite.svg (generated from design/glyphs/mark-*.svg). Rows draw them at 20 px. On rows the unlit half is the trackRow token (rule D): dark tinted 60% toward the lit colour, light the role’s track; high contrast draws it in GrayText. Finished is neutral at every size: the 16 px cut is a 4 px dot, the 24 px cut a native check. In every theme, background is a dotted S in Active (3:1 or better on every row) and delegating a small S with three orbit dots, so neither depends on colour to differ from working. Connecting and offline stand the halves apart, offline dimmed to 85%, which keeps 3:1 on every row.
