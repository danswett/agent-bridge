PROPOSAL #536 · SLIPGATES BRAND

Decided · App-icon plate colour

Decided 2026-11-03 One plate on every platform. Shown at 96, 48 and 32 on a dark and a light desktop, and among other icons on a phone.

ChosenA · Ink plate, white mark

Ink #0E1114 with a white mark, the brand exploration’s pick. The icon carries no status colour, so a teal or blue S on a row never reads as “the app”. Strongest on light desktops; on dark ones the edge keeps the tile. tokens.json’s appIcon now carries it: plate #0E1114, edge #2A2F36, mark #FFFFFF.

B · Deep teal plate, Bridge mark (the earlier tokens)

What the generator drew before the decision: #0F1F22 with the Bridge mark. Ties the icon to the accent, but the teal S sits close to the status Ss and the plate reads near-black at small sizes.

C · Bridge plate, ink mark

A teal plate with an ink mark: the loudest on a home screen, and the easiest to find. It spends the accent on the icon, and themed or tinted icons lose the most.
