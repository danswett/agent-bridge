PROPOSAL #536 · SLIPGATES BRAND

The mark

Approved 2026-11-03 The v7 S: two linked gates. It is the app icon, the title-bar mark, and the base of every status glyph.

Construction (v7)

Drawn from design/icons/mark.svg. Blue: centre lines. Amber: the 40-unit live area. Violet: the badge seat and its clearance.

What the construction says

Two gates, one link
The S is two identical hooks, each a bar that turns into a gate. One is the other turned 180° about the centre: two endpoints and the passage between them.
Grid
48 units. Bars 4.75 thick with 0.9 corners, slanted 18°. The hooks meet the centre line (blue) and leave the corners open.
Clear space
The mark’s live area is 40 of 48 (amber). Keep the box’s 4-unit margin empty, and at least ¼ of the mark’s height between it and anything else.
Badge seat
A state badge (finished, driven) sits on the 5.6 circle at 40.5, 40.5 with 2.4 clearance (violet). The S never reaches inside it, so no badge needs a cut.

Small cuts: hand-placed outlines, not the display mark scaled down

At 24 px and under, use design/icons/mark-16, -20 and -24: every bar lands on whole pixels. From 30 px up, use the display mark.

Minimum sizes, on the canvas

Minimum 16 px (the native cut). Under 16 px, use no mark at all. Status glyphs in rows: 20 px.
