PROPOSAL #536 · SLIPGATES BRAND

Wordmark and lockup

Name approved Typeface decided The mark and the name side by side, in the interim face.

Construction

Slipgates
x = ¼ mark
  • The mark’s box is the lockup’s height. The name’s cap height is half of it, centred on it.
  • Gap between mark and name: 0.3 of the mark. Clear space all round: ¼ of the mark (x).
  • “Slipgates”: one word, capital S. Real faces only; Inter Tight SemiBold until Slipgates Sans (#544) lands.
  • The mark takes Bridge; the name takes the text colour. Never a status colour.

On surfaces

Sizes

The lockup’s minimum is a 20 px mark. Below that, or in app chrome (title bars, app bars), set the name in the platform’s UI face beside the mark: the wordmark is for splash, about, store and docs.
