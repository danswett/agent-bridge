Auto-scroll, two columns and cancelling

The list scrolls under a card held near its edge, the same make-room motion works in a grid, and a drop outside the list puts the card back.

Auto-scroll speed
v = 1000 px/s × (depth into the zone ÷ zone)²
Zone 10% of the list’s height, at least 40 px and at most 80 px, at the top and the bottom.
Curve quadratic: a quarter of the speed halfway in, so a card held just inside the zone creeps and one held at the edge races.
Arming only after the card has moved 4 px since the lift, so lifting a card that already sits in the zone doesn’t scroll.
Pointer outside the list (above or below) scrolls at full speed.
4 · The speed curve

Frame by frame, not by timer: each frame scrolls v × the frame’s time, and fractions carry over.
